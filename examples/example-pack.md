# Example: a short filled pack (invented candidate)

Everything here is invented to show the format: the candidate (Dana), the companies and the numbers. A real pack has 25 outlines of 150 to 220 words each; this shows the numbers sheet, three shortened outlines and two questions to ask.

**Role:** Senior SEO / AI search lead, remote. **Closest posting:** an invented B2B SaaS company that asks for "an AI workflow you built and how you checked it".

**Sources:** `resume/dana-2026.pdf`, `resume/dana-2025.pdf`, `portfolio/case-studies.md`, the posting, notes from the 12 March screening call. Every number below comes from those files.

## Outlines

### 9. How do you measure AI visibility?

- **S:** In 2026 I built a baseline for my own side project across ChatGPT, Perplexity and Gemini.
- **T:** Find out whether "ranking in ChatGPT" is even a number you can track.
- **A:** 60 buyer questions from Search Console and support tickets, no brand names. Each run three times per engine, one run a day on three days: 540 answers. Logged whether the site was named and whether it was cited, per engine. Search Console AI Overviews impressions and GA4 AI referrals in separate rows.
- **R:** Perplexity cited it 18% of the time, ChatGPT 6%, Gemini 4%. Same site, three very different numbers, which is why I never blend them.

**"Rate, not rank, and each engine on its own row."**

### 13. Tell me about a time AI got something wrong in your work.

- **S:** 2025, Northfield. An AI draft of a help article for a new feature.
- **T:** Publish it before the launch.
- **A:** Read it against the product before it went out. It described a setting the feature didn't have. Cut it, and changed the process: every AI draft now gets checked against the release notes, and a product person approves anything about new features.
- **R:** The article went out on time and correct, and the check is still in the brief template.

**"The fix wasn't the edit. It was the step that catches the next one."**

### 23. Our clicks dropped but rankings held. What do you do?

No client story for this one: it's a method question.

- **S:** Same average position, fewer clicks.
- **T:** Find out whether an AI Overview or a SERP change took the click before anyone rewrites pages.
- **A:** 16 months of Search Console page by page; pages that held position but lost CTR; check those queries by hand for AI Overviews; then titles and first lines written to win the click or the citation. Track 28 days against the baseline.
- **R:** No number to quote. Say what you'd expect to learn and when.

**"Same rank, fewer clicks usually means the answer moved above you."**

# 30-60-90 day plan: AI search lead (frame)

Works for any company because the first month is spent learning theirs. Bring it as one page; adjust the nouns on the call. For a non-search role, keep the shape (read, baseline, first fixes, re-measure, hand over) and swap the contents.

## Days 1 to 30: read, get access, set the baseline

- **Week 1 is reading.** Their docs, product, past SEO reports, the last audit, sales calls or support tickets if they'll share them. Nothing on the site changes yet.
- **Access on day one:** Search Console, GA4, Bing Webmaster Tools, the CMS or repo, server or CDN logs, the ad account, one point person, and who decides what.
- **Build the question set.** 30 to 50 buyer questions in customers' words, from Search Console queries, People Also Ask, sales and support language. No brand-name questions in the core set.
- **Run the baseline.** Every question in ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Google AI Overviews / AI Mode, several runs each, on more than one day. Log appearance, citation, which URLs get cited, and what the answer says about the brand.
- **Technical check:** can AI crawlers reach the site (robots.txt, CDN bot rules, logs), does key content render without JavaScript, is the schema valid and true to the page, are the money pages indexed.
- **Measurement plumbing:** a GA4 channel group for AI referrals, AI Overviews impressions from Search Console, one dashboard where AI and Google sit in separate rows.
- **Day 30 deliverable:** the baseline report and a ranked roadmap, agreed with the manager before anything deploys.

## Days 31 to 60: deploy the first fixes, and the workflow behind them

- **Engineering tickets:** indexing blockers, template schema, rendering problems. Each ticket says what done looks like.
- **Content:** answer-first rewrites on the 10 pages closest to money, each with one quotable sentence and a number worth citing. New pages only where nobody's answer is good.
- **Off-site:** the cited third-party pages from the baseline (listicles, directories, review sites, Reddit threads), sorted into get listed, pitch, or ignore.
- **Entity clean-up:** the brand described the same way on the site, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, directories and review sites.
- **Workflow:** the monthly run and the audit become written procedures (agent skills or the team's equivalent), with a person approving anything that publishes. The team can run it without the hire.
- **Weekly written update** from week one: what went live, what moved, what's next, what's stuck.

## Days 61 to 90: re-measure, report honestly, plan the next quarter

- **Re-run the full question set** in the dated window. Report appearance and citation rates per engine against the baseline, next to Google numbers, never blended.
- **Say what moved and what didn't,** and where cause can't be separated from noise.
- **Double down** on what moved; drop what didn't.
- **Hand over the playbook:** question set, run procedure, tracking sheet, ticket templates.
- **Next-quarter plan** with targets set from their own baseline, not a vendor's benchmark.

**Day 90 success, in their terms:** a baseline they trust, the first fixes live, a monthly AI visibility number leadership reads, and a process that doesn't depend on one person being online.
